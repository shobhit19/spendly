Privacy Policy

How Spendly collects, uses and protects your information

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and a password, which is stored in hashed form. When you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to sign you in, to record your expenses, and to show you summaries and breakdowns of your spending. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in a database managed by Spendly. We take reasonable steps to protect it from unauthorised access, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can edit or delete your expense entries at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you visit our pages. We do not share your account or expense data with any other third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team through the contact details provided on our website.
